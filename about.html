About LEmandi

We started LEmandi because homeowners deserve stylish products without the inflated price tag.

Why we started

Two Bad Options, So We Built a Third

Renovating or furnishing a home in Malaysia usually means choosing between two extremes: pay retail markup for a trusted name, or gamble on a cheaper unknown. We didn't think homeowners should have to choose.

LEmandi was built to close that gap. A home living showroom that sources directly from manufacturers and passes the savings straight to you, without cutting corners on quality or style. We're a young showroom based in Balakong, and we're building LEmandi the same way we build our product range: one honest decision at a time.
