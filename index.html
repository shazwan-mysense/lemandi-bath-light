About us

A One-Stop Home Living Showroom

LEmandi is a one-stop bathroom, kitchen and lighting showroom in Balakong, offering sanitary ware, bathroom fittings, kitchen sinks, cooker hoods, water heaters, ceiling fans and home improvement essentials at warehouse-direct prices. That's what “Barang Stylo, Harga Power” means to us: premium goods, honest and affordable prices.

Renovating a home in Malaysia usually means choosing between two extremes: pay retail markup for a trusted name, or gamble on a cheaper unknown. We built LEmandi to close that gap. We source directly from manufacturers and pass the savings straight to you, without cutting corners on quality or style. Today we serve homeowners, renovators and contractors across Klang Valley, Kuala Lumpur and Selangor.
